# Calorie Tracker — Business Concept and Historical Investor Pitch

**Independent venture · Indonesia · Pre-launch development in 2025**

This document provides business context for the [Calorie Tracker AI Android prototype](../README.md), based on a 12-slide investor pitch presentation prepared for the original venture. The project was **discontinued before product launch**. The pitch was a proposal, not a record of shipped features, investment secured, user traction, or realized revenue.

## Problem and product idea

The concept was motivated by increasing attention to nutrition and healthy habits in Indonesia and challenges with user retention in fitness apps. The proposed solution combined calorie tracking with a more enjoyable daily routine:

- **Conversational food logging:** estimate calorie intake from natural-language descriptions of meals
- **Virtual animal companion:** a gamified user experience to encourage repeat engagement
- **Localized food database:** a proposed catalog tailored to Indonesian meals and eating habits
- **AI-based recommendations:** use LLMs for assisted meal logging and feedback

## Commercialization work

The proposal considered an Indonesian target market, product positioning, market-size estimates, subscription pricing, investor funding needs and technical staffing. Its financial tables include **hypothetical forecasts and assumptions**. They were not validated by actual paying users, product launch data or investment results. Forecast figures and third-party statistics were included in the historical presentation and have not been independently audited here.

## Project outcome

The venture reached prototype development and pitch preparation but was **discontinued before launch**. The value of this repository is evidence of independent Android development, API integration, product exploration, and business planning—not a claim of a launched startup.
